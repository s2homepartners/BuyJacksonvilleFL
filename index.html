About S² Home Partners

Sarah Luther & Sarah Fuller

S² Home Partners is a real estate partnership formed by Northeast Florida Realtors® Sarah Luther and Sarah Fuller. Serving Jacksonville, Yulee, Fernandina Beach, and surrounding communities, the duo brings over 13 combined years of experience to every transaction. They combine strategic marketing, strong local knowledge, dynamic listing solutions, and client-first guidance to help buyers and sellers navigate every step of the real estate process.

Sarah Luther

Real Estate Advisor / Realtor®

Specialties: Strategic home marketing, first-time homebuyers, military moves, and buyer/seller representation.

Real estate advisor and veteran spouse with personal experience navigating military relocations. Sarah is known for her detail-oriented approach to property evaluations, responsive communication, and commitment to building long-term relationships within the community.

Sarah Fuller

Real Estate Advisor / Realtor®

Specialties: Relocation specialist, first-time home buyers, and full-service buyer/seller advocacy.

As a military spouse who has managed moves across the globe, Sarah brings empathy, adaptability, and firsthand relocation knowledge to her clients. She focuses on creating a seamless transaction experience, helping families find homes that match their specific lifestyle and financial goals.
